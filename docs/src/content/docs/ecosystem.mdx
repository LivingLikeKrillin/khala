title: Ecosystem
description: How the tools connect, only through Khala.
---

The producer tools never call each other directly. They connect **only through Khala**: one link, not a web of point-to-point wires.

<img
  src="/diagrams/ecosystem.svg"
  alt="Developers and agents reach Archon, the authority window; Archon, Arbiter, and Observer publish to and query Nexus — the Khala link. The tools connect only through Khala."
  style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1.5rem auto;"
/>

- **Archon** is the single authority window: people and agents come to it for domain truth.
- **Observer** is one client of that window, alongside developers.
- **Arbiter** publishes approved specs into Khala, but it isn't the source of truth; the authoritative frontmatter is.
- **Adept** reads the same link from the other side: the warehouse is the denominator of cognitive debt — what must be known — and vouch coverage is measured against it.
